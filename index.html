About the Franchise

The idea for the movie was created in 2024 by Samuel Jaskiewicz. He wanted to make a kung fu movie like no other. We were sick of movies that were too young and babyish, and movies that were rated too high. We created RFA as the perfect movie with the perfect rating - Positively Great. With backing from his brothers, S. Jaskiewicz formed Flightless Studios, but they only had 3 people. They elisted the help of Guntur Studios and brought them on as co-producers. They movie had a rough start, with early difficulties in the screenwriting phase. Once the script was somewhat finalized, props were made and filming locations secured. Filming took 6 months, and eventually the footage was edited and ready for screening. The premiere was a grand sucess, although the movie didn't make any money at the box office and the profits were negative. After the premiere was the Oscars awards, and RFA 1 won 6, taking the ceremony by storm.
